Ki 9 Kyrdan jong ka Jingduwai, Lectio Divina & Marian Devotion

Ki 9 kyrdan tynrai jong ka Jingduwai

1

Vocal Prayer

Ka jingduwai da ki kyntien: Ko Kpa jong ngi, Psalms, Rosary, liturgy, petition bad praise. Ka dohnud ka dei ban pyniasoh bad ki kyntien kiba ngi ong.

2

Meditation

Ka jingpyrkhat jylliew halor Jingthoh Bakhuid, ka jingim U Khrist, doctrine bad ki mystery jong ka jingngeit, ban pynlong ia ka jingshisha sha ka jingieit bad resolution.

3

Affective Prayer

Ka reasoning ka nang long simple bad ka mynsiem ka jubab kham kloi da ka jingieit, repentance, praise, thanksgiving bad surrender.

4

Prayer of Simplicity

Ka jingpeit da ka jingieit kaba suk sha U Blei ne sha kawei ka jingshisha jong ka jingngeit; ki jingmut kiba bun ki duna bad ka jingpynleit jingmut ka long kham shitylli.

5

Infused Contemplation

U Blei u sdang ban leh kham paw ha ka mynsiem. Ka loving knowledge kam mih tang na human method hynrei la pdiang kum ka jingaiei.

6

Prayer of Quiet

Ka mon ka shah ring jylliew ha U Blei ha ka jingsuk, watla kiwei ki faculty ki lah ban dang treikam ne shah pynwit.

7

Prayer of Simple Union

Ki faculty hapoh ki nang shah lum lang ha U Blei bad ka mynsiem ka mad ia ka infused union kaba kham jylliew.

8

Conforming Union

Ha Teresian language la pyniasoh bad spiritual betrothal. U soh uba kongsan ym dei extraordinary experience hynrei ka jingpyniasoh ia ka mon jong ngi bad ka mon U Blei.

9

Transforming Union

La khot ruh spiritual marriage: ka union kaba skhem jong ka mon bad U Blei, kaba paw da mature charity, humility bad apostolic fruitfulness.

St. Teresa of Ávila — Ki 7 Mansions jong ka Interior Castle

St. Teresa ka ianujor ia ka mynsiem bad ka iing syiem kaba long kum ka crystal castle, kaba don shibun ki kamra bad U Syiem u shong ha pdeng. Ki 7 mansions kim dei tang prayer techniques; ki batai ia ka jingiaid lynti jong ka conversion, self-knowledge, purification, infused prayer bad transforming union.

First Mansions — Ka jingrung sha Castle

Ka mynsiem ka sdang serious conversion bad jingduwai hynrei ka dang shah ktah jur da ka pop, distraction bad worldly attachment. Teresa ka ban jur ia self-knowledge bad humility.

Second Mansions — Ka jingiohsngew ia ka jingkhot

U briew u nang sngap ia U Blei lyngba preaching, good friendship, spiritual reading, suffering bad grace. Ki temptation ki dang khlain hynrei perseverance ha ka jingduwai ka long kaba kongsan.

Third Mansions — Ka jingim Khristan kaba ryntih

Ka jingim kaba serio ha morality ka nang mih: jingduwai, sacramental life, charity bad avoidance of grave sin; hynrei self-reliance kaba rieh ka dang donkam purification.

Fourth Mansions — Ka jingsdang Mystical Prayer

Teresa ka pyniapher acquired recollection bad supernatural prayer. Hangne ka mynsiem ka lah ban sdang pdiang infused recollection bad prayer of quiet.

Fifth Mansions — Prayer of Union

Teresa ka pyndonkam ia ka dur jong u silkworm uba kylla butterfly. Ka union bad U Blei ka dei ban pynmih self-forgetting love, service ha ka Balang bad jinglong kloi ban shah shitom na ka bynta U Khrist.

Sixth Mansions — Spiritual Betrothal & Purification

Ki jingaiei kiba khraw ki lah ban wan ryngkat bad ki jingtynjuh, misunderstanding, illness bad interior purification. Extraordinary phenomena kim long kiba kongsan ban ia virtue bad fidelity.

Seventh Mansions — Spiritual Marriage

Ka union kaba jylliew bad kaba skhem; ka Trinitarian indwelling ka shah mad kham shai. Ki soh shisha ki long service, humility, courage, love of the Church bad willingness to labor for souls.

Lectio Divina — Ka jingduwai bad ka Ktien U Blei

Kaei ka Lectio Divina? Lectio Divina ka mut “divine reading” — ka rukom tynrai jong ka Balang ban pule Jingthoh Bakhuid ha ka rukom jingduwai, khnang ba ka Ktien U Blei kan long ka jingïakynduh bad U, ka jingkylla kaba mut bad ka jingim.

Ka rukom kaba la paw ha ka tradition jong ka Balang ka iaid lyngba Lectio, Meditatio, Oratio, Contemplatio, bad ka kut ha Actio — kata, ka Ktien U Blei ka kylla sha ka jingim bad ka jingleh.

1

Lectio — Pule

Jied ka bynta kaba lyngkot na Jingthoh Bakhuid, khamtam na Gospel ne ki reading jong ka sngi. Khot ia U Mynsiem Bakhuid ban ai jingshai. Pule suki bad kylli: Kaei kaba ka text ka ong shisha?

2

Meditatio — Pyrkhat jylliew

Sah bad ka kyntien ne verse kaba ktah ia phi. Kylli: Kaei kaba U Blei U kren sha nga lyngba kane ka text?

3

Oratio — Duwai

Jubab ia U Blei da ka dohnud: praise, thanksgiving, repentance, petition, intercession ne surrender.

4

Contemplatio — Shong jar ha U Blei

Haba ki kyntien ki nang duna, shong jar ha ka jingïadon jong U Blei khlem pynbor ia kano kano ka extraordinary experience.

5

Actio — Leh ia ka Ktien

Rah ia ka Ktien sha ka jingim: jingkylla kaba mut, jingmap, jingleh ieit, jingkohnguh bad jingshakri.

Kumno ban discern ia ka jingroi ha ka jingduwai

Wat beh ia ki experience. Consolation, visions ne unusual sensations kim dei ki reliable measure jong ka holiness.

Peit ia ki soh: charity, patience, truthfulness, chastity, mercy, forgiveness, sacramental fidelity, humility bad obedience.

To sah ha ka Balang: Scripture, Liturgy, Confession, Eucharist, sound doctrine bad competent spiritual guidance ki long ka environment ba bha.

Pdiang ia ka dryness. Ka jingbymdon sensible consolation kam mut ba ka jingduwai ka fail.
