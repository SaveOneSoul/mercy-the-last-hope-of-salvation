Ki Eucharistic Miracle & ka Theology Katholik

Ki jaka ba la pyniasoh bad ki Eucharistic miracle, ki link sha ki jaka bad ka jinghikai halor ka Eucharist na Jingthoh Bakhuid, St. Thomas Aquinas, CCC, Church Fathers, Vatican II bad ki Pope.

Ka nongrim Katholik: ka jingngeit ha ka Real Presence kam shong ha ki extraordinary phenomena. Ki Eucharistic miracle ki lah ban long devotional signs; hynrei ngi ngeit ha ka Eucharist namar ki kyntien U Khrist, ka Apostolic faith bad ka sacramental teaching jong ka Balang.

Ka Theology Katholik halor ka Eucharist

1. Ka nongrim na Jingthoh Bakhuid

Last Supper: Mt 26:26–28; Mk 14:22–24; Lk 22:19–20. U Jisu u pyni ia u kpu bad ka waiñ kum ka Met bad ka Snam jong U bad u hukum, “Leh ia kane kum ka jingkynmaw ia Nga.”

Bread of Life: Jn 6:22–71 ka batai ia U Khrist kum U Kpu jong ka Jingim bad ka jingdonkam ban iasoh bad U.

Apostolic witness: 1 Cor 10:16–17 ka kren shaphang participation ha ka Met bad Snam U Khrist; 1 Cor 11:23–29 ka ai ia ka institution narrative bad maham pyrshah ia ka unworthy reception.

Catechism

CCC 1322–1419 ka hikai ia ka Eucharist kum thanksgiving, memorial, sacrifice, Real Presence, sacramental communion bad pledge jong ka burom kaban wan. CCC 1324 ka khot ia ka Eucharist “source and summit of the Christian life.”

Read CCC

Vatican II

Lumen Gentium §11 ka ong ba ka Eucharistic sacrifice ka dei ka source bad summit jong ka jingim Khristan. Sacrosanctum Concilium ka hikai ia ka Liturgy kum ka summit bad source jong ka bor ka Balang.

Transubstantiation

Ka Balang ka hikai ba ha ka consecration, ka substance jong u kpu ka kylla sha ka Met U Khrist bad ka substance jong ka waiñ sha ka Snam jong U, katba ki appearances ki dang sah. Peit CCC 1376.

St. Thomas Aquinas halor ka Eucharist

Ha Summa Theologiae III, q.73–83, Aquinas u batai ia ka institution, matter bad form, conversion jong u kpu bad waiñ, ka Real Presence, ki effect jong Holy Communion bad ka Mass.

Real Presence

U Khrist u don shisha bad substantially, ym tang symbolically. Ka jingkylla ka jia ha ka substance katba ki accidents ki dang sah.

Summa III q.75

U Khrist Baroh

Namar U Khrist ba la mihpat um lah ban phiah, U Khrist baroh u don hapoh man ka species bad man ka bynta jong ka species.

Ki soh mynsiem

Ka Eucharist ka pynbam ia ka charity, pyniasoh kham jylliew bad U Khrist, pynkhlain ecclesial unity bad iarap pyrshah ia ka pop.

Ka sakhi jong ki Church Fathers

St. Ignatius of Antioch

Ha ka letter sha Smyrnaeans, Ignatius u pyni ba ka orthodox faith ka ithuh ia ka Eucharist kum ka doh U Jisu Khrist.

St. Justin Martyr

Justin u batai ia ka Sunday Eucharistic assembly bad ong ba ki Khristan kim pdiang ia ka Eucharistic food kum u common bread bad drink.

St. Cyril of Jerusalem

U kyntu ia kiba dang shah baptize ban shaniah ha ki kyntien U Khrist halor ka Met bad Snam, ym tang ha ki senses.

St. Augustine

U pyniasoh ka Eucharistic communion bad ka ecclesial communion: pdiang ia ka Mystery jong ka Met U Khrist bad long kata kaba phi pdiang.
