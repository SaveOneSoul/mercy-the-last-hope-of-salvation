Communion of Saints · Ki Sakhi U Khrist

Ki Riewkhuid jong ka Balang

Ka searchable Khasi directory jong ki riewkhuid, ki feast day bad ki short story.

Ka note: ka Roman Martyrology ka don da ki hajar ngut ki riewkhuid bad blessed. Kane ka Khasi directory ka dang iai roi; ki profile kiba don hangne ki long hapoh ka website hi, khnang ba ka navigation kam shaniah ha ki external saint URLs kiba lah ban kylla.

St. Joseph

Feast: March 19

U lok jong Ka Maria Theisotti bad foster father U Jisu. Ka Gospel ka pyni ia u kum u briew uba hok, uba kohnguh kloi ia U Blei bad uba iada ia ka Holy Family.

St. Peter the Apostle

Feast: June 29

U nongtong dohkha uba U Khrist u khot ban ialam ia ki Twelve. Hadien ka jinglen jong u, U Jisu u pyniohpat ia u bad u kylla long u sakhi ba kongsan jong ka Resurrection bad u shepherd jong ka Balang ha Rome.

St. Paul the Apostle

Feast: June 29

Saul uba ju pynshitom ia ki Khristan u la shah pynkylla da U Khrist ba la mihpat bad u kylla long u missionary bakhraw sha ki Gentile bad nongthoh bun tylli ki New Testament letters.

St. Mary Magdalene

Feast: July 22

Ka disciple U Jisu kaba sah hajan ka Diengphna bad kaba long ka sakhi ba nyngkong jong U Trai ba la mihpat ha ka Gospel u John.

St. Stephen

Feast: December 26

Uwei na ki first seven deacons bad u martyr ba nyngkong jong ka Balang, uba la sakhi da ka jingshlur bad map ia kiba pyniap ia u.

St. Ignatius of Antioch

Feast: October 17

U bishop bad martyr jong ka Early Church uba ki letter jong u ki sakhi jur ia ka unity bad u bishop, ka Eucharist bad ka fidelity sha U Khrist.

St. Justin Martyr

Feast: June 1

U philosopher bad apologist ha ka second century uba iada ia ka Christianity bad ieh ia kawei na ki description ba rim tam jong ka Sunday Eucharistic assembly.

St. Augustine of Hippo

Feast: August 28

Hadien bun snem jong ka jingwad, Augustine u la kylla kaba mut bad shah baptize; hadien u kylla bishop jong Hippo bad uwei na ki theologian kiba ktah jur ia ka Balang.

St. Monica

Feast: August 27

Ka kmie u Augustine, kaba la kynmaw kyrpang namar ka jingduwai ba iaineh na ka bynta ka conversion jong u khun bad ka Christian motherhood kaba shahniah.

St. Francis of Assisi

Feast: October 4

U khun nongkhaii uba pdiang ia evangelical poverty, seng ia ka Franciscan family bad iarap pynthymmai ia ka Balang lyngba repentance, joy bad love of Christ.

St. Clare of Assisi

Feast: August 11

Ka follower jong St. Francis kaba seng ia ki Poor Clares bad im ha radical poverty, Eucharistic devotion bad contemplative prayer.

St. Dominic

Feast: August 8

U nongfounder jong Order of Preachers uba pyniasoh contemplation, study bad preaching na ka bynta ka jingshisha bad ka jingpynim ia ki mynsiem.

St. Thomas Aquinas

Feast: January 28

U Dominican theologian bad Doctor of the Church uba ka synthesis jong faith bad reason ka la pynwandur jur ia Catholic theology, khamtam halor ka Eucharist.

St. Catherine of Siena

Feast: April 29

Ka Dominican tertiary, mystic bad Doctor of the Church kaba shakri ia kiba pang, kyntu ia ka reform bad trei na ka bynta ecclesial unity.

St. Teresa of Ávila

Feast: October 15

Ka Carmelite reformer bad Doctor of the Church kaba hikai jylliew halor ka mental prayer, contemplative life bad ka seven mansions jong ka Interior Castle.

St. John of the Cross

Feast: December 14

U Carmelite mystic bad Doctor of the Church uba hikai halor purification, dark night bad union of love bad U Blei.

St. Thérèse of Lisieux

Feast: October 1

Ka Carmelite nun bad Doctor of the Church kaba hikai ia ka “Little Way” jong ka jingshaniah, humility bad ka jingieit ha ki kam barit.

St. Faustina Kowalska

Feast: October 5

Ka Polish religious sister kaba Diary jong ka ka long ka source ba kongsan jong ka modern Divine Mercy devotion, kaba khot ia ka pyrthei sha trust ha U Jisu bad mercy toward others.

St. John Paul II

Feast: October 22

U Pope uba la ialam ia ka Balang naduh 1978–2005, pynbna jur ia ka dignity jong u briew, Divine Mercy, New Evangelization bad ka theology of the body.

St. Teresa of Calcutta

Feast: September 5

Ka founder jong Missionaries of Charity kaba shakri ia kiba duk tam, kiba pang bad kiba la shah iehnoh, da kaba iohi ia U Khrist ha “the distressing disguise of the poor.”

St. Alphonsa

Feast: July 28

Ka Franciscan Clarist sister na Kerala bad ka kynthei ba nyngkong na India ban shah canonize; ka jingim jong ka ka pyni ia ka patient offering of suffering ha U Khrist.

St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara

Feast: January 3

U Indian priest bad founder uba la kyntiew ia ka education, printing, religious life bad renewal ha ka Syro-Malabar Church.

St. Devasahayam Pillai

Feast: January 14

U lay Catholic Indian martyr jong ka 18th century uba la shah pynshitom namar ka jingngeit bad iaineh ha U Khrist haduh ka jingiap.

St. Carlo Acutis

Feast: October 12

U samla Catholic uba pyndonkam ia ka computer skill ban pynbna ia ka Eucharist bad catalogue ia ki Eucharistic miracle traditions, bad uba im da ka daily Mass, prayer bad charity.

St. Pio of Pietrelcina

Feast: September 23

U Capuchin priest uba pawnam namar ka ministry of Confession, prayer, suffering bad ka jingkhot ia ki briew sha repentance bad trust in God.

St. Maximilian Kolbe

Feast: August 14

U Franciscan priest bad Marian apostle uba aiti la ka jingim ha Auschwitz ban iap ha ka jaka jong uwei u kpa jong ka longiing.

St. Francis Xavier

Feast: December 3

U Jesuit missionary uba pynbna ia ka Gospel ha India bad Asia bad uba long uwei na ki patron saint jong ka missionary work.
