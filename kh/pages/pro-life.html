Ka nongrim: ka burom jong u briew

Ka Balang ka iada ia ka jingim jong u khun ha kpoh namar ba ka burom jong u briew kam wan na ka sorkar, ka spah, ka koit ka khiah, ka rta ne ka jingkwah jong kiwei. Ka jingim briew ka dei ka jingai U Blei bad la khot ia u briew sha ka jingiasoh bad U.

Namar kata, u khun ha kpoh, ka kmie, u kpa, kiba pang, kiba duna dkhot, kiba duk bad kiba tymmen ki dei ban ioh ka burom bad ka jingsumar. Ka rukom Pro-Life kaba Catholic ka iada ia ka jingim bad ha kajuh ka por ka ieng ryngkat bad kiba shem jingeh.

Ka jingsneng Katholik halor ka jingpynhap khun

Ka Catechism ka hikai ba ka jingim briew ka dei ban ioh ka burom bad ka jingiada naduh ka por ba ka jingim ka sdang ha kpoh. U khun bym pat kha u dei ban ioh ka burom kaba dei hok jong u briew.

Namarkata, ka Balang ka hikai ba ka direct abortion — kata ka jingpynhap khun kaba la thmu kum ka thong lane kum ka lad ban ioh kawei pat ka thong — ka long kaba pyrshah jur ia ka hukum jong U Blei.

Ka burom jong u khun kam kylla namar ba u long uba koit ne uba duna dkhot, uba la thmu ne bym pat la thmu, uba duk ne uba riewspah.

Ki jingiapher kiba kongsan ha ka moral theology

Ka moral theology Catholic ka pyniapher hapdeng ka direct abortion bad ka jingsumar medical ia ka jingpang kaba jur jong ka kmie, haba ka jingiap jong u khun kam dei ka thong bad kam dei ruh ka lad kaba la jied. Ki medical case kiba jwat ki donkam doctor kiba tbit bad, lada lah, ka jingiasyllok Catholic bioethics.

Canon Law halor ka procured abortion

Canon 1397 §2: ka Code of Canon Law kaba mynta ka ong ba uba actually procures an abortion, haba ka abortion ka la jia shisha, u hap ha ka latae sententiae excommunication. Kane ka penalty jong ka Balang ka pyni ia ka jingkhia jong ka kam kaba pynkut ia ka jingim jong uba lui-lui ha kpoh.

Hynrei kam mut ba baroh kiba don bynta ha ka abortion ki shah automatically ha kajuh ka canonical penalty ha man la ka circumstance. Canon Law ka peit ia ka jingkitkhlieh shisha jong u briew. Canons 1321–1324 ki kren shaphang ka rta, ka jinglah ban pyrkhat, ki jait jingbymtip, grave fear, necessity bad kiwei ki circumstance kiba lah ban pynduna lane pyndam ia ka canonical penalty. Namarkata ym dei ban bishar ia u briew tang na shabar khlem ka jingtip ia ka case.

Pope Francis ha Misericordia et Misera §12 u la ai ha baroh ki lyngdoh ka faculty ban absolve ia ka pop jong ka procured abortion. U la pynskhem ar tylli ki jingshisha: abortion ka long ka pop kaba jur namar ka pynkut ia ka jingim ba lui-lui, hynrei ym don ka pop kaba kham khlain ban ia ka Jingisynei U Blei haba u nongpop u kylla kaba mut bad u wad ka jingmap.

Hato abortion ka dei “ka pop kaba pyrta sha bneng”?

Ka Catechism §1867 ka kdew ia ki “sins that cry to heaven”: ka snam u Abel, ka pop jong ki Sodomite, ka jingpyrta jong ki briew ba la banbei ha Egypt, ka jingpyrta jong u nongwei, ka riewkynthei bad u khunswet, bad ka jingbymhok ia u nongtrei ha ka bainong.

Ka Catechism kam ong kyrpang ha §1867 ba “abortion” ka dei kawei na kine. Namar kata, ngim dei ban ai ka citation kaba bakla. Hynrei ka Balang ka hikai ha kawei pat ka bynta ba direct abortion ka long ka pop kaba jur namar ka thombor ia ka jingim ba lui-lui. Ka dur jong ka snam u Abel kaba “pyrta” sha U Blei (Genesis 4:10) ka lah ban iarap ban sngewthuh ia ka jingkhia jong ka innocent bloodshed.

Ka kyntien rim “crying to heaven for vengeance” dei ban sngewthuh kum ka jingpyrta sha U Blei na ka bynta ka hok bad ka justice, ym kum ka jingbit ia u briew ban pynhiar kput, ban leh jubor ne ban pynmynsaw.

Ka Jingisynei, ka jingkylla kaba mut bad ka jingpynkhiah

Ym don mano-mano uba palat ia ka Jingisynei U Blei. Ka Balang ka pyrshah ia abortion, hynrei kam ai jingbit ban niewbei, ban pynjah burom ne ban iehnoh ia ka kynthei, u kpa lane kino-kino kiba la shah ktah.

Kiba rah ia ka jingrem, jingsngewsih ne jingmong hadien abortion ki dei ban shah ialam sha U Khrist. Ka Balang ka ai ka lad jong ka repentance, Sacramental Confession, spiritual accompaniment bad ka jingkyrmen jong ka reconciliation.

Ka jingduwai na ka bynta ka jingim

Ko Trai Jisu Khrist, Nongthaw jong ka jingim bad Tyllong jong ka Jingisynei, iada ia uwei pa uwei u khun ha kpoh, pynkhlain ia ki kmie kiba sheptieng ne kiba shem jingeh, ai ka jingkitkhlieh ha ki kpa, ka jingstad ha ki longiing bad ki doktor, bad pynkylla ia ki dohnud kiba la sngew ba ka jingim briew kam don dor. Pynkhiah ia kiba la shah pynmong da ka abortion, ialam ia ki sha ka Jingisynei jong Me, bad pynlong ia ka Balang jong Me ka jaka jong ka jingshisha, ka jingiada, ka jingisynei bad ka jingkyrmen. Amen.
