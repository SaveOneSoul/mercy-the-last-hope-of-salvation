Ka mission

“Save One Soul” ka dei ka campaign evangelization kaba tei halor ka intercessory prayer, ka jingkñia, ki kam jingisynei, ka jingbud lang bad ka jinglam ia ki briew sha ka jingiadei kaba im bad U Jisu Khrist bad ka Balang jong U. Ym don u briew uba lah ban guarantee mechanically ia ka jingpynim jong uwei pat; ka jingpynim ka dei ka jingaiei U Blei, kaba U tyrwa ei bad kaba u briew u pdiang da ka jinglaitluid.

1. Duwai

Duwai man ka sngi na ka bynta uwei u briew da kaba kdew kyrteng, khamtam na ka bynta ka jingkylla kaba mut, jingpynkhiah, jingiasuk bad final perseverance.

2. Aiti

Pyniasoh ki jingkñia barit, fasting, jingjynjar bad ki kam jingisynei bad ka Passion jong U Khrist na ka bynta ka bha mynsiem jong kiwei.

3. Synran

Sngap khlem pynbor; sakhi ia U Khrist; khot sha ka jingduwai, Jingthoh Bakhuid, Confession, Mass Bakhuid bad ka community.

Ka jingkular ha Diary na ka bynta ka conversion jong ki nongpop

Ha Diary §§186–187, St. Faustina ka thoh ba U Jisu u khot ia ka ban khot kyrpad ia ka Jingisynei jong U na ka bynta ki nongpop namar U kwah ia ka jingpynim jong ki.

U Jisu u pyniasoh ia kawei ka jingkular bad ka jingduwai harum haba la duwai “with a contrite heart and with faith on behalf of some sinner”: U ong, “I will give him the grace of conversion.”

Kane ka mut ba ym dei tang ka jingpynbud kyntien. U nongduwai u dei ban wan da ka jingngeit, ka dohnud kaba kylla kaba mut bad da ka jingthmu shai ban iasaid na ka bynta u nongpop, da kaba aiti ia u ha ka Jingisynei U Jisu.

Kum ka private revelation, kane kam bujli ia ka Gospel, ki Sakrament, repentance, human freedom ne ka jingsneng paidbah jong ka Balang halor ka jingpynim.

Ka jingduwai na ka bynta ka conversion jong u nongpop

“O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fount of Mercy for us, I trust in You.”

Diary §187

Kumno ban duwai na ka bynta uwei

  1. Buhteng ha ka dohnud ia u briew uba phi duwai na ka bynta jong u.
  2. Sngewrit ha khmat U Blei bad phla ba ma ngi ruh ngi donkam ia ka Jingisynei jong U — kane ka dei ka mynsiem jong ka contrition.
  3. Duwai da ka jingngeit ha U Jisu Khrist bad ka jingshaniah ha ka Jingisynei jong U.
  4. Ong ia ka jingduwai halor da kaba aiti kyrpang ia uta u briew.
  5. Aiti lut ia u ha U Blei khlem pynbor, khlem jingsheptieng bad khlem pyrshang ban control ia ka result.

Ki kyntien “O Blood and Water” ki pynkynmaw ia ka krung jong U Khrist kaba la dung (Jn 19:34) bad ki pynleit ia ka jingshaniah sha U Jisu bad ka Passion ba pynim jong U.

Ki 33 Acts of Trust — Diary §341

Ka jingiapher kaba kongsan. Ka jingkular conversion ha Diary §§186–187 ka iadei bad ka jingduwai “O Blood and Water” da ka jingngeit bad contrition na ka bynta u nongpop. Ha kawei pat ka bynta, Diary §341 ka thoh ba St. Faustina ka duwai ia kajuh ka invocation 33 sien na ka bynta ka intention jong u Holy Father. Namarkata, 33 kam dei ka condition jong ka jingkular conversion.

33 repetitions

Duwai suki bad da ka jingshaniah:

O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fount of Mercy for us, I trust in You.

Pynbud 33 sien.

Ka bor jong ka jingduwai kam shong ha u number kum ka magic formula. Ki repetitions ki dei ki acts jong ka jingshaniah kaba iai neh.

Ki kyntien halor ka jingpynim ia ki mynsiem

Diary §1032

Ka jingsliang U Jisu na ka bynta ki mynsiem ka pyniasoh bad ka Passion; Faustina ka shah khot ban iarap da ka jingduwai bad ka jingaiti jingsuffering.

Diary §1397

Ka jingduwai na ka bynta ka conversion jong ki nongpop ka long kaba pynsngewbha kyrpang ia U Jisu bad ka ai jingpyntngen ia ka Dohnud jong U.

Diary §1690

Faustina ka iohi ia ka jingduwai bad jingkñia kum ki lad kiba kongsan ban iatreilang bad U Khrist na ka bynta ki mynsiem.

Ka simple 7-day Save One Soul practice

  1. Jied uwei u briew bad buhrieh ia ka intention.
  2. Duwai “O Blood and Water” na ka bynta jong u da ka jingngeit bad contrition.
  3. Aiti shi decade jong ka Rosary man ka sngi.
  4. Duwai Divine Mercy Chaplet shisien hapoh ka taiew.
  5. Aiti kawei ka kam self-denial, service ne mercy.
  6. Kyrpad ia U Mynsiem Bakhuid ia ka jingstad shuwa ban kren ne leh.
  7. Aiti lut ia uta u briew ha U Jisu khlem pressure, manipulation ne anxiety.
