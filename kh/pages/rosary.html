Ka Rosary Bakhuid

Baroh 20 tylli ki Mystery, ki jingduwai ba ju pyndonkam bad ka Litany of Loreto.

Ka Rosary Catholic: ki Joyful Mystery (Monday/Saturday), Luminous (Thursday), Sorrowful (Tuesday/Friday) bad Glorious (Wednesday/Sunday).

Kumno ban sdang

Ka dak ka Diengphna

Ha ka kyrteng U Kpa, bad U Khun, bad U Mynsiem Bakhuid. Amen.

Ka Apostles’ Creed

Nga ngeit ha U Blei U Kpa Uba donbor baroh, Nongthaw ka bneng bad ka khyndew; bad ha U Jisu Khrist, U Khun ba marwei jong U, U Trai jong ngi; uba la pun da U Mynsiem Bakhuid, uba la kha na Ka Maria Theisotti, uba la shah shitom hapoh Pontius Pilate, shah sahnarphna, iap bad shah tep; U la hiar sha ki jaka kiba shapoh; ha ka sngi kaba lai U la mihpat na kiba iap; U la kiew sha bneng bad U shong ha ka kamon U Blei U Kpa; nangta Un wan ban bishar ia kiba im bad kiba iap. Nga ngeit ha U Mynsiem Bakhuid, ka Balang Katholik Bakhuid, ka communion jong ki riewkhuid, ka jingmap ia ki pop, ka jingmihpat jong ka met bad ka jingim bymjukut. Amen.

Ko Kpa jong ngi

Ko Kpa jong ngi uba ha bneng, long bakhuid ka kyrteng jong Me; wan ka hima jong Me; long ka mon jong Me ha khyndew kumba long ha bneng. Ai ha ngi mynta ka jingbam kaba ngi donkam; bad map ia ki pop jong ngi kumba ngi map ia kiba leh pop ia ngi; bad wat ialam ia ngi sha ka jingpynshoi, hynrei pynlait ia ngi na kaba sniew. Amen.

Khublei Maria

Khublie Maria, kaba dap da ka jingaiei, U Trai u don ryngkat bad pha. Pha long kaba kyrkhu hapdeng ki kynthei, bad uba kyrkhu u soh jong ka kpoh jong pha, U Jisu. Ko Maria Bakhuid, Kmie U Blei, duwai na ka bynta jong ngi ki nongpop, mynta bad ha ka por ka jingiap jong ngi. Amen.

Ka burom

Ka burom ha U Kpa, bad ha U Khun, bad ha U Mynsiem Bakhuid; kumba ka long naduh kaba sdang, mynta bad junom, bymjukut. Amen.

Fatima Prayer

Ko Jisu jong nga, map ia ki pop jong ngi, pynlait ia ngi na ka ding ka dujok, ialam ia baroh ki mynsiem sha bneng, khamtam ia kiba donkam eh ia ka Jingisynei jong Me. Amen.

Ki 20 Mystery

Joyful Mysteries Monday & Saturday

1. Ka Annunciation — Lk 1:26–38
2. Ka Visitation — Lk 1:39–56
3. Ka jingkha ia U Jisu — Lk 2:1–20
4. Ka Presentation ha Temple — Lk 2:22–38
5. Ka jinglap ia U Jisu ha Temple — Lk 2:41–52

Luminous Mysteries Thursday

1. Ka Baptism U Jisu ha Jordan — Mt 3:13–17
2. Ka Wedding ha Cana — Jn 2:1–11
3. Ka jingpynbna ia ka Hima bad ka jingkhot sha repentance — Mk 1:14–15
4. Ka Transfiguration — Mt 17:1–8
5. Ka jingpynlong ia ka Eucharist — Mt 26:26–29

Sorrowful Mysteries Tuesday & Friday

1. Ka Agony ha Garden — Mt 26:36–46
2. Ka Scourging at the Pillar — Mt 27:26
3. Ka Crowning with Thorns — Mt 27:27–31
4. Ka jingbah ia ka Diengphna — Lk 23:26–32
5. Ka Crucifixion bad ka jingiap U Jisu — Jn 19:17–37

Glorious Mysteries Wednesday & Sunday

1. Ka Resurrection — Lk 24:1–12
2. Ka Ascension — Acts 1:6–11
3. Ka jinghiar U Mynsiem Bakhuid — Acts 2:1–13
4. Ka Assumption jong Ka Maria — cf. Rev 12:1; Catholic Tradition
5. Ka Coronation jong Ka Maria kum Ka Syiem ka Bneng bad Khyndew — cf. Rev 12:1; Catholic Tradition

Ha man ka Mystery: 1 Ko Kpa jong ngi · 10 Khublei Maria · Ka Burom · optional Fatima Prayer.

Ki jingduwai pynkut

Hail Holy Queen

Khublie, Ko Syiem Bakhuid, Kmie ka Jingisynei, ka jingim, ka jingsngewtynnad bad ka jingkyrmen jong ngi. Sha pha ngi pyrta, ngi ki khun ba la shah beh jong ka Im; sha pha ngi phah ia ki jingud bad jingsngewsih jong ngi ha kane ka them jong ki ummat. Phai, Ko Nongiasaid ba isynei, ia ki khmat jingisynei jong pha sha ngi; bad hadien kane ka jingiaid nongwei, pyni ha ngi ia U Jisu, u soh ba kyrkhu jong ka kpoh jong pha. Ko Maria kaba jem, kaba ieit bad kaba thiang. Duwai na ka bynta jong ngi, Ko Kmie Bakhuid U Blei, ba ngin long kiba bit ban ioh ia ki jingkular U Khrist. Amen.
