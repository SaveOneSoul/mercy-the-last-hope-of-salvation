Ka Theology jong U Mynsiem Bakhuid

U Mynsiem Bakhuid um dei tang ka bor, feeling ne spiritual energy. U long U Person Blei uba lai jong ka Trinity Bakhuid Tam, U Blei shisha ryngkat bad U Kpa bad U Khun: “U Trai, U Nongai Jingim.” U Kpa u phah ia U Mynsiem, bad U Khrist u phah ia U Mynsiem ha ka economy of salvation.

U Mynsiem u pynpaw ia U Khrist, pynmih jingngeit, ai ka jingkha thymmai, pynkhuid ia kiba la baptize, shong hapoh kiba ngeit, tei ia ka Balang bad ai charisms na ka bynta ka mission. Ki charism kim dei private possessions; ki dei ki gifts na ka bynta ka common good bad ki donkam discernment.

Jingthoh Bakhuid: Jn 3:5–8; Jn 14:16–17,26; Jn 15:26; Jn 16:7–15; Acts 1:8; Acts 2; Rom 5:5; Rom 8:14–27; 1 Cor 12–14; Gal 5:22–25.

Ka Theology jong Baptism in the Holy Spirit

Kam dei ka eighth sacrament. Ha Catholic Charismatic theology, Baptism in the Holy Spirit kam bujli bad kam repeat ia sacramental Baptism ne Confirmation.

La sngewthuh ia ka kum ka prayerful bad experiential actualization bad renewal jong ka baptismal bad confirmation grace: ka surrender kaba kham jylliew sha U Jisu kum U Trai, ka renewed awareness ia ka jingieit U Kpa, repentance, empowerment for witness, jingthrang ia ka prayer bad Scripture, love for sacraments bad openness sha charisms.
