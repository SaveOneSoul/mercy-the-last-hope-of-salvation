Seven Sorrows of Our Lady

A Gospel-centered guide to the Seven Sorrows of Mary, with Scripture references and careful distinction between biblical text and devotional tradition.

The Seven Sorrows devotion contemplates Mary's participation in the suffering connected with Christ's mission and Passion. It should always lead the believer more deeply into the Gospel and the Paschal Mystery.

Sorrow 1

The prophecy of Simeon

Luke 2:34–35

Sorrow 2

The flight into Egypt

Matthew 2:13–15

Sorrow 3

The loss of the Child Jesus in Jerusalem

Luke 2:41–52

Sorrow 4

Mary meets Jesus on the way of the Cross

Meditation within the Passion tradition

Sorrow 5

Mary stands at the foot of the Cross

John 19:25–27

Sorrow 6

Jesus is taken down from the Cross

Meditation on the deposition

Sorrow 7

Jesus is laid in the tomb

John 19:38–42

A simple way to pray

For each sorrow: announce the sorrow, read the associated Scripture where available, pause in silence, pray one Our Father if desired, and pray seven Hail Marys or another approved form according to the devotional tradition you follow.

Source discipline: some details in popular versions of this devotion come from later devotional tradition rather than directly from a single biblical verse. This site distinguishes explicit Scripture references from traditional meditations.
