The Holy Bible

Explore all 73 books of the Catholic Bible and study Scripture according to the principles of Dei Verbum.

The Catholic Bible contains 73 books: 46 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament. Vatican II's Dei Verbum teaches Catholics to receive Sacred Scripture within the living Tradition of the Church and to attend carefully to what the inspired human authors intended to express.

Four principles for Catholic Bible study

  1. Read the text in its literary and historical context.
  2. Read individual passages within the unity of the whole of Scripture.
  3. Read within the living Tradition of the Church.
  4. Respect the analogy of faith: the coherence of the truths of faith with one another.
