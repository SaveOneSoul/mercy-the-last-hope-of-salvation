Holy Rosary — Four Mysteries

Pray and study the Joyful, Luminous, Sorrowful and Glorious Mysteries of the Holy Rosary.

The Rosary is a Christ-centered Marian prayer. St. John Paul II described it as a contemplative path in which the believer looks upon Christ with Mary. Use the tabs below to explore all four sets of mysteries.

Joyful Mysteries

Usually Monday & Saturday

  1. The Annunciation
  2. The Visitation
  3. The Nativity of Jesus
  4. The Presentation of Jesus in the Temple
  5. The Finding of Jesus in the Temple

Luminous Mysteries

Usually Thursday

  1. The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan
  2. The Wedding at Cana
  3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom and the Call to Conversion
  4. The Transfiguration
  5. The Institution of the Eucharist

Sorrowful Mysteries

Usually Tuesday & Friday

  1. The Agony in the Garden
  2. The Scourging at the Pillar
  3. The Crowning with Thorns
  4. The Carrying of the Cross
  5. The Crucifixion and Death of Jesus

Glorious Mysteries

Usually Wednesday & Sunday

  1. The Resurrection
  2. The Ascension
  3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
  4. The Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary
  5. The Coronation of Mary

Basic Rosary sequence

  1. Sign of the Cross and Apostles' Creed.
  2. Our Father, three Hail Marys, Glory Be.
  3. Announce each mystery, then pray one Our Father, ten Hail Marys and the Glory Be while meditating on the mystery.
  4. After five decades, conclude with customary prayers such as the Hail Holy Queen.
