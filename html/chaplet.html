Chaplet of Divine Mercy

A clear, step-by-step Catholic guide for praying the Chaplet of Divine Mercy on Rosary beads.

The Chaplet of Divine Mercy is prayed on ordinary five-decade Rosary beads. The sequence below follows the widely used form presented by the National Shrine of The Divine Mercy.

Prayer text note: This page provides the standard short-form sequence for devotional use. For optional opening and closing prayers and approved devotional material, follow the source link below.

How to pray the Chaplet

  1. Make the Sign of the Cross.
  2. Pray one Our Father.
  3. Pray one Hail Mary.
  4. Pray the Apostles' Creed.
  5. On each large bead:
    Eternal Father, I offer You the Body and Blood, Soul and Divinity of Your dearly beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, in atonement for our sins and those of the whole world.
  6. On each of the ten small beads:
    For the sake of His sorrowful Passion, have mercy on us and on the whole world.
  7. After five decades, pray three times:
    Holy God, Holy Mighty One, Holy Immortal One, have mercy on us and on the whole world.

Pray with an intention

Offer the Chaplet for the Church, peace, sinners, the suffering, the dying, families, those who have asked for prayer, and the needs of the whole world.
