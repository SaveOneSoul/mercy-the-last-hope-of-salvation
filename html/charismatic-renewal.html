Catholic Charismatic Renewal

Learn about Catholic Charismatic Renewal as a current of grace centered on the Holy Spirit, prayer, evangelization, unity, charisms and service.

Catholic Charismatic Renewal is commonly described by CHARIS as a “current of grace” rather than a single worldwide movement with one founder. Its expressions include prayer groups, communities, ministries, evangelization initiatives and service to the poor.

Praise & worship

Prayer of praise, thanksgiving, adoration and expectant openness to the Holy Spirit.

Charisms

Openness to spiritual gifts for the building up of the Church, exercised with discernment and charity.

Evangelization

Proclamation of Jesus Christ, personal conversion, testimony, Scripture and missionary discipleship.

Unity & service

CHARIS highlights Christian unity and service to the poor as key dimensions of its mission.

CHARIS and the wider Church

CHARIS is the Catholic Charismatic Renewal International Service. Vatican sources record the establishment of CHARIS as a new single service for the diverse expressions of Catholic Charismatic Renewal, and recent papal addresses continue to call the Renewal toward evangelization, communion and service.

For this website, charismatic spirituality is always presented inside Catholic sacramental, biblical and ecclesial life—not as an alternative Church or as a spirituality independent of the bishops and the Magisterium.

Prayer group health checks

  • Christ-centered proclamation and fidelity to Catholic teaching.
  • Regular sacramental life, especially Eucharist and Reconciliation.
  • Respect for parish and diocesan authority.
  • Discernment of prophecy, healing claims and extraordinary manifestations.
  • Transparent leadership and safeguarding of vulnerable people.
  • No financial manipulation, personality cults or pressure tactics.
