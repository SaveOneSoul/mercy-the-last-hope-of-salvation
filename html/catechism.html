Catechism of the Catholic Church

Explore major Catholic doctrines by CCC paragraph range together with biblical references. The summaries on this website are study aids; the official Catechism remains the authoritative text.

Reference policy: Mercy AI may cite only CCC paragraph ranges and Scripture references present in the approved reference map or other approved Catholic sources. It must not invent paragraph numbers or biblical citations.
CCC 50–141; especially 74–100 and 101–141

Divine Revelation and Scripture

Catholic faith receives one deposit of faith through Sacred Tradition and Sacred Scripture, authentically interpreted by the Magisterium. Scripture is inspired and read within the Church.

Scripture: 2 Timothy 3:14–17, 2 Thessalonians 2:15, Luke 24:44–45, John 20:30–31

CCC 232–267

The Holy Trinity

The central mystery of Christian faith is one God in three divine Persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Scripture: Matthew 28:19, 2 Corinthians 13:13, John 1:1–18, John 14:16–17

CCC 279–324

Creation and Providence

God freely created all that exists and continually sustains and guides creation by divine providence.

Scripture: Genesis 1:1–31, Psalm 104, John 1:1–3, Colossians 1:15–17

CCC 385–421

The Fall and Original Sin

The Church teaches the reality of the Fall and original sin while proclaiming Christ as the new Adam whose grace surpasses sin.

Scripture: Genesis 3:1–24, Romans 5:12–21, 1 Corinthians 15:21–22

CCC 422–682

Jesus Christ, Son of God and Savior

Jesus Christ is true God and true man, the incarnate Son who died and rose for our salvation.

Scripture: John 1:1–18, Philippians 2:5–11, 1 Corinthians 15:1–8, Colossians 1:15–20

CCC 484–511; 963–975

The Blessed Virgin Mary

Mary is Mother of God and exemplary disciple; Catholic Marian doctrine is always ordered to Christ and the mystery of the Church.

Scripture: Luke 1:26–56, Luke 2:1–35, John 2:1–11, John 19:25–27, Acts 1:14

CCC 748–945

The Church

The Church is the People of God, Body of Christ and Temple of the Holy Spirit, confessed as one, holy, catholic and apostolic.

Scripture: Matthew 16:18–19, Acts 2:42–47, 1 Corinthians 12:12–27, Ephesians 2:19–22

CCC 946–962

Communion of Saints and Intercession

All who belong to Christ share communion in holy things and among holy persons; the saints intercede for the Church.

Scripture: Hebrews 12:1, Revelation 5:8, Revelation 8:3–4, James 5:16

CCC 1076–1209; especially 1113–1134

The Sacramental Economy

Christ acts in the Church's liturgy and sacraments, efficacious signs of grace entrusted to the Church.

Scripture: John 1:14, John 20:21–23, Matthew 28:19, 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

CCC 1213–1284

Baptism

Baptism is the foundation of Christian life, freeing from sin, giving new birth and incorporating the baptized into Christ and the Church.

Scripture: Matthew 28:19, John 3:5, Acts 2:38, Romans 6:3–4, 1 Peter 3:21

CCC 1285–1321

Confirmation

Confirmation completes baptismal grace by a special outpouring of the Holy Spirit and strengthens Christians for witness.

Scripture: Acts 8:14–17, Acts 19:5–6, 2 Corinthians 1:21–22, Hebrews 6:1–2

CCC 1322–1419; especially 1373–1381

The Eucharist and Real Presence

The Eucharist is the source and summit of Christian life; Christ is truly, really and substantially present under the Eucharistic species.

Scripture: John 6:51–58, Matthew 26:26–28, Luke 22:19–20, 1 Corinthians 10:16–17, 1 Corinthians 11:23–29

CCC 1422–1498

Penance and Reconciliation

Christ entrusted the ministry of reconciliation to the Church; the sacrament includes contrition, confession, satisfaction and priestly absolution.

Scripture: John 20:21–23, 2 Corinthians 5:18–20, James 5:16, Luke 15:11–32

CCC 1499–1532

Anointing of the Sick

The Anointing of the Sick strengthens Christians facing serious illness or frailty and unites them more closely to Christ's Passion.

Scripture: James 5:14–15, Mark 6:13, Luke 10:34

CCC 1536–1600

Holy Orders

Holy Orders is the sacrament through which the apostolic ministry continues in bishops, priests and deacons.

Scripture: Luke 22:19, John 20:21–23, Acts 6:1–6, 1 Timothy 4:14, 2 Timothy 1:6

CCC 1601–1666

Sacrament of Matrimony

The matrimonial covenant establishes a lifelong partnership between a baptized man and woman and is ordered to the good of the spouses and the generation and education of children.

Scripture: Genesis 2:18–24, Matthew 19:4–6, John 2:1–11, Ephesians 5:21–33

CCC 1691–2051; Beatitudes 1716–1729

Christian Moral Life and Beatitudes

Christian morality flows from life in Christ, grace, the Beatitudes, virtue and a conscience formed in truth.

Scripture: Matthew 5:1–12, Romans 12:1–2, Galatians 5:13–26, Philippians 4:8

CCC 2052–2557

Ten Commandments

The Decalogue is fulfilled in the law of love taught by Christ and remains a fundamental expression of moral life.

Scripture: Exodus 20:1–17, Deuteronomy 5:6–21, Matthew 19:16–22, Matthew 22:37–40

CCC 2558–2865

Christian Prayer

Prayer is the living relationship of God's children with the Father, through the Son, in the Holy Spirit, expressed in blessing, petition, intercession, thanksgiving and praise.

Scripture: Matthew 6:5–13, Luke 11:1–13, Romans 8:26–27, Philippians 4:6–7, 1 Thessalonians 5:17

CCC 683–747; gifts and fruits 1830–1832; charisms 799–801

Gifts and Fruits of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit sanctifies the Church, bestows gifts and charisms, and forms the fruits of Christian life.

Scripture: Acts 2:1–4, Isaiah 11:2–3, 1 Corinthians 12:4–11, Galatians 5:22–23

CCC 65–67

Public and Private Revelation

Public Revelation is complete in Christ; approved private revelations may help believers live the faith more fully but do not add to the deposit of faith.

Scripture: Hebrews 1:1–2, John 16:13, Jude 3

CCC 1846–1876; grace 1987–2029

God's Mercy, Sin and Conversion

God's mercy confronts sin with saving love and calls the sinner to conversion, forgiveness, justification and life in grace.

Scripture: Luke 15:11–32, John 3:16–17, Romans 5:8, Ephesians 2:4–10, 1 John 1:8–9

CCC 1030–1032

Purgatory and Prayer for the Dead

Those who die in God's grace and friendship but still need purification undergo a final purification; the Church prays for the dead.

Scripture: 2 Maccabees 12:44–46, 1 Corinthians 3:12–15, Matthew 12:32

CCC 880–887

Peter and the Petrine Ministry

The Pope, Bishop of Rome and successor of Peter, has a unique ministry of unity within the college of bishops and the whole Church.

Scripture: Matthew 16:18–19, Luke 22:31–32, John 21:15–17
