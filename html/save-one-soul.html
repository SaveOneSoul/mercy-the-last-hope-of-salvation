Save One Soul Campaign

A prayerful, non-coercive Catholic evangelization pathway built on witness, accompaniment, mercy and respect for human freedom.

Save One Soul Campaign is designed here as a respectful Catholic evangelization movement: pray for people, witness to Christ with integrity, invite without coercion, accompany patiently, and entrust conversion to God's grace.

🙏

1. Pray

Choose one person or family and pray consistently for grace, healing, faith and freedom.

👂

2. Listen

Learn the person's story. Evangelization is not a sales script; respect freedom, wounds, doubts and timing.

🕯️

3. Witness

Share what Christ and the Church mean in your life without exaggerating, manipulating or arguing.

🚪

4. Invite

Invite the person to Mass, Eucharistic Adoration, a prayer meeting, Scripture group, parish event or conversation with a priest.

🤝

5. Accompany

Stay present after the invitation. Conversion and discipleship normally require friendship, catechesis and community.

❤️

6. Serve

Make mercy visible through concrete service, forgiveness, generosity and concern for the poor and vulnerable.

Prayer commitment

Lord Jesus Christ, place one soul upon my heart. Teach me to pray without judging, to speak truth with charity, to serve without seeking recognition, and to trust the work of Your grace. Make me an instrument of Your mercy. Amen.
