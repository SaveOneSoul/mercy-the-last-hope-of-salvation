Pages of Divine Mercy

A resource hub for Divine Mercy Sunday, the Hour of Mercy, the novena, St. Faustina and deeper Catholic study.

This page is a Divine Mercy resource hub. It points visitors toward sound Catholic material rather than reproducing entire copyrighted books or the full Diary of St. Faustina.

Feast

Divine Mercy Sunday

Celebrated on the Second Sunday of Easter, the feast keeps the Paschal Mystery at the center: the risen Christ comes to His disciples with peace, forgiveness and the wounds of His saving Passion.

Prayer

Hour of Great Mercy

The traditional three o'clock remembrance directs attention to Christ's Passion. It may be observed with a brief prayer, meditation on the Passion, the Chaplet, or other fitting devotion.

Nine days

Novena of Chaplets

A novena is nine days of prayer. The National Shrine of The Divine Mercy provides guidance for a Divine Mercy Novena using the Chaplet.

The Diary is an important devotional text associated with the mission of Divine Mercy. This site will offer summaries, themes and references rather than copying long sections.
