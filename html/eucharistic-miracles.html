Eucharistic Miracles

A sober Catholic introduction to Eucharistic miracles, the Real Presence and responsible verification of extraordinary claims.

Catholic belief in the Eucharist does not depend on extraordinary phenomena. The Church's faith rests on Christ's institution of the Eucharist, Sacred Scripture, Apostolic Tradition and sacramental doctrine. Reports of Eucharistic miracles should therefore be presented carefully and never sensationalized.

Lanciano

A historic Italian tradition associated with an extraordinary Eucharistic event. Visitors should consult diocesan and reputable Catholic documentation rather than viral summaries.

Orvieto / Bolsena

A medieval tradition closely connected in Catholic memory with Eucharistic devotion and the feast of Corpus Christi.

Modern documented cases

Claims from different countries vary in evidentiary quality and ecclesial status. This site will add only entries with clear sourcing and status labels.
