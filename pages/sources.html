Mariology, Psychology & Counselling

Lesson explanations and fictional case exercises are original introductory study material. Each lesson links its readings. Mariology draws on Scripture, the Catechism, Vatican II and current DDF guidance, including Mater Populi fidelis (2025). Psychology readings include OpenStax Psychology 2e; care resources include WHO, NIMH and the BACP Ethical Framework. These organisations do not endorse this site or its courses.

Internal course credits and certificates are non-accredited and do not confer professional qualifications, registration or permission to practise. The AI support tools are not clinical or emergency services. Their privacy and data-processing information appears before sending a message.
