Mercy Marian Prayer Library

Original Catholic prayers for everyday needs, written for Mercy – The Last Hope of Salvation and formed by the Church’s teaching on Mary, prayer and the communion of saints.

Christ remains the one Mediator and Redeemer. Catholic prayer to Mary asks for her maternal intercession; it never gives Mary the adoration due to God alone. Genuine Marian devotion leads us to Jesus Christ, to His Word, to the sacraments and to a life of conversion and charity.

Traditional Marian prayers of the Church

Rather than reproducing official texts from other websites, this library points directly to the Holy See for established Marian prayers and devotions.

Hail Mary

The Church’s most familiar Marian prayer, rooted in the Gospel greetings of Gabriel and Elizabeth and completed by the Church’s petition for Mary’s intercession.

Angelus

A daily meditation on the Incarnation that joins Marian prayer to the mystery of the Word made flesh.

Regina Caeli

The Easter-season Marian antiphon that rejoices in the Resurrection of Christ.

Holy Rosary

A contemplative prayer centered on Christ’s mysteries and prayed in communion with Mary.

Litany of Loreto

A traditional litany recommended by the Church, combining praise of Mary with repeated requests for her prayer.

Sub Tuum Praesidium

One of the Church’s most ancient Marian invocations, asking the Mother of God for maternal protection in need.

Mary Help of Christians

A Catholic title of confidence and mission. Mary Help of Christians points to Mary as Mother who accompanies the Church, families, the young, the sick and those who suffer, always directing them toward her Son.

Prayer to Mary Help of Christians

Mary, Help of Christians, Mother of Jesus and Mother of the Church, take us under your maternal care. When our faith is weak, pray that we may cling to Christ. When our homes are troubled, obtain patience, forgiveness and peace. When young people are confused, lead them toward truth, courage and holiness. When the sick and elderly suffer, remain close to them and bring them the consolation of your Son. When the Church is opposed or discouraged, strengthen her children in fidelity and charity. Teach us to listen to Jesus and to do whatever He tells us. Mary, Help of Christians, pray for us. Amen.

Prayers for everyday needs

For a Special Intention

Mary, Mother of Jesus, I place before you this intention that weighs upon my heart. Carry my prayer with your maternal intercession to your Son. Ask for the grace that is truly good for my salvation and for those involved. If the answer is different from what I expect, help me trust the Father’s wisdom. Keep me faithful, patient and open to God’s will. Amen.

In Anxiety and Uncertainty

Holy Mary, Mother of God, when fear scatters my thoughts and I cannot see the road ahead, pray that Christ may give me His peace. Help me remember that I am held by the Father, accompanied by the Holy Spirit and never abandoned by the Lord. Obtain for me prudence for today and trust for tomorrow. Amen.

Against Temptation and Human Weakness

Mary, Virgin most faithful, pray for me when temptation is strong and my will is weak. Lead me quickly to Jesus. Obtain for me purity of heart, honesty, self-mastery and the courage to reject what separates me from God. Help me seek Confession when I fall and never despair of Christ’s mercy. Amen.

For Work and Study

Mary, Seat of Wisdom, intercede for me in my work and study. Ask the Holy Spirit to give me clarity, discipline, honesty and perseverance. Keep me from laziness, pride and discouragement. May what I learn and accomplish be used in service of truth, family, Church and neighbor. Amen.

Prayers for family, children and young people

For Family Unity

Mary, Mother of the Holy Family, pray for our home. Where there is anger, obtain patience; where there is hurt, obtain forgiveness; where there is distance, obtain sincere conversation; where there is fear, obtain trust in God. Help spouses be faithful, parents be wise and children grow in grace. Keep our family close to Jesus in prayer and charity. Amen.

For Young People

Mary, Help of Christians, accompany every young person searching for identity, friendship and purpose. Protect them from exploitation, addiction, despair and false promises. Pray that they may encounter Jesus personally, discover their vocation and use their gifts with courage. Give them good friends, wise guides and a heart capable of truth and generous love. Amen.

For a Child Who Has Drifted from the Faith

Mary, Mother of hope, I entrust to you this child who has grown distant from prayer, the sacraments or the Church. Do not let my fear become bitterness or control. Pray that the Holy Spirit may work gently and powerfully in this life. Give me patience, good example, wise words and persevering love. Lead us all closer to Jesus. Amen.

For Vocations

Mary, Mother of the Church, pray for young men and women whom Christ is calling to priesthood, religious life, marriage and generous Christian service. Obtain for them freedom from fear, purity of intention and courage to answer God. Pray also for families and communities to support authentic vocations with faith and joy. Amen.

Prayers for life, sickness, grief and healing

For Expectant Mothers and Unborn Children

Mary, Mother of the Lord who carried Jesus beneath your heart, pray for every expectant mother and every child in the womb. Obtain protection in danger, practical help in poverty, courage in fear and faithful support from fathers and families. May every child be welcomed as a gift and every mother be treated with tenderness and dignity. Amen.

For the Sick

Mary, Health of the Sick, remain near those who suffer in body or mind. Present their needs to Jesus, the Divine Physician. Obtain strength for the patient, wisdom for doctors and nurses, patience for caregivers and peace for families. If healing is delayed, ask that no suffering be wasted and that hope in Christ remain alive. Amen.

For Those Who Grieve

Our Lady of Sorrows, you stood beneath the Cross and knew the pain of separation. Stay close to those who mourn. Help them entrust the departed to the mercy of God, receive the support of the Church and endure the empty places left by loss. Lead them toward the hope of the Resurrection and eternal life in Christ. Amen.

For Healing after Abortion

Mary, Mother of Mercy, accompany those who carry sorrow, guilt or wounds after abortion. Lead them without fear to Jesus, whose mercy is greater than every sin. Obtain courage for repentance, grace to receive sacramental reconciliation, healing of memories and compassionate support from the Church. May despair give way to hope and new life in Christ. Amen.

Prayers for the Church, peace and mission

For the Church

Mary, Mother of the Church, pray for the Pope, bishops, priests, deacons, religious and all the faithful. Keep the Church faithful to the Gospel, courageous in truth, humble in service and merciful toward sinners. Protect her from division, scandal and discouragement. Help every parish become a home of prayer, holiness and mission. Amen.

For Peace

Mary, Queen of Peace, intercede for peoples wounded by war, hatred and injustice. Ask Christ to convert hearts that choose violence, comfort civilians and refugees, protect children and strengthen peacemakers. Teach nations to seek justice without revenge and reconciliation without forgetting truth. May the peace of Christ begin in our own homes and hearts. Amen.

For Evangelization

Mary, Star of Evangelization, pray for all who proclaim Jesus Christ. Obtain humility for preachers, courage for missionaries, wisdom for catechists and charity for every Christian witness. Keep us from seeking attention for ourselves. May our words, works and lives lead others toward repentance, faith, the sacraments and friendship with your Son. Amen.

For the Save One Soul Mission

Mary, Mother of Mercy, place before Jesus every person who feels far from God. Pray for the conversion of sinners, the return of those who have left the Church and the healing of those wounded by sin or scandal. Make us instruments of patient love rather than judgment. Help us pray, sacrifice, serve and speak the truth so that even one soul may rediscover the mercy of Christ. Amen.
