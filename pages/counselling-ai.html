Privacy before you send

Your message and up to eight recent conversation messages are sent through Mercy’s server to OpenAI for safety checks and a response. Mercy’s chat endpoint does not write transcripts to a database or application logs. OpenAI may retain information under its data policies, including for abuse monitoring; this is not a confidential clinical service.

The conversation remains in this page’s memory until you clear or leave it. Other people using your device may see the open page. Clearing it cannot retract data already transmitted. Optional goal memory saves only the selected category, not messages or check-ins.

When you need a person

Immediate danger or urgent medical need: call local emergency services. In India, call 112. If safe, ask someone trusted nearby to stay with you.

Mental-health support in India: Tele-MANAS · 14416. Outside India, contact your local mental-health or crisis service. This website cannot call for help or monitor your safety.

For ongoing support, contact a qualified local counsellor, psychologist or doctor. Ask about credentials, methods, fees, confidentiality and availability. If you are under 18, reach a trusted adult or suitable youth service; if home is unsafe, seek another safe adult or emergency help.
