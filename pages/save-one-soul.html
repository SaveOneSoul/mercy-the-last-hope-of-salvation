The mission

“Save One Soul” is an evangelization campaign built around intercessory prayer, sacrifice, works of mercy, patient accompaniment and bringing people toward a living relationship with Jesus Christ and His Church. No human being can mechanically guarantee another person’s salvation. Salvation is God’s grace, freely offered and freely received; the Christian cooperates through prayer, witness, sacrifice and love.

1. Pray

Intercede daily for one person by name, especially for conversion, healing, reconciliation and final perseverance.

2. Offer

Unite ordinary sacrifices, fasting, suffering and works of mercy to Christ’s Passion for the spiritual good of souls.

3. Accompany

Listen without manipulation; witness to Christ; invite the person to prayer, Scripture, Confession, Holy Mass and Christian community.

The Diary promise for the conversion of sinners

In Diary §§186–187, St. Faustina records Jesus calling her to invoke His mercy on behalf of sinners because He desires their salvation.

Jesus attaches a specific promise to the prayer below when it is offered “with a contrite heart and with faith on behalf of some sinner”: He says, “I will give him the grace of conversion.”

This means the emphasis is not merely on reciting words. The intercessor is invited to pray in faith, with genuine contrition before God, and deliberately on behalf of a particular sinner, entrusting that person to the mercy of Jesus.

As private revelation, this promise does not replace the Gospel, the sacraments, repentance, human freedom or the Church’s ordinary teaching on salvation. It is received as a devotional encouragement to trust more deeply in Christ’s mercy.

Prayer for a sinner’s conversion

“O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fount of Mercy for us, I trust in You.”

Diary §187

How to pray it for someone

  1. Call to mind the person for whom you are interceding.
  2. Humble yourself before God and acknowledge your own need for mercy — this is the spirit of contrition.
  3. Pray with faith in Jesus Christ and confidence in His mercy.
  4. Say the prayer above specifically on behalf of that person.
  5. Entrust the person to God without manipulation, fear or an attempt to control the outcome.

The words “O Blood and Water” recall the pierced side of Christ (John 19:34) and direct the act of trust to Jesus, whose saving Passion is the source of mercy.

The 33 Acts of Trust — Diary §341

Important distinction. The conversion promise in Diary §§186–187 concerns praying the “O Blood and Water” prayer with faith and contrition on behalf of a sinner. Separately, Diary §341 records St. Faustina praying the same invocation 33 times for the intention of the Holy Father. The number 33 is therefore not a condition attached to the conversion promise.

33 repetitions

For the devotional practice connected with Diary §341, recollect yourself before Christ and pray:

O Blood and Water, which gushed forth from the Heart of Jesus as a fount of Mercy for us, I trust in You.

Repeat 33 times.

The repetitions are acts of persevering trust, not a magical formula. When praying for conversion, the essential disposition highlighted in §§186–187 is faith and contrition on behalf of the sinner.

Words for the salvation of souls

Diary §1032

Jesus’ thirst for souls is joined to His Passion; Faustina is called to assist through prayer and the offering of suffering for sinners.

Diary §1397

Prayer for the conversion of sinners is presented as especially pleasing to Jesus and as a source of consolation to His Heart.

Diary §1690

Faustina understands prayer and sacrifice as central means by which her mission cooperates in bringing souls to Christ.

A simple 7-day Save One Soul practice

  1. Choose one person and keep the intention confidential.
  2. Pray the “O Blood and Water” prayer for that person with faith and contrition.
  3. Offer one decade of the Rosary each day.
  4. Pray the Divine Mercy Chaplet during the week.
  5. Offer one concrete act of self-denial, service or mercy.
  6. Ask the Holy Spirit for wisdom before speaking or acting.
  7. Entrust the person completely to Jesus, without pressure, manipulation or anxiety.
