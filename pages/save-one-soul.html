Save One Soul Campaign

Pray, sacrifice, accompany and witness to Christ with mercy.

Campaign anchor: “I thirst. I thirst for the salvation of souls. Help Me, My daughter, to save souls.” Diary §1032

The mission

“Save One Soul” is an evangelization campaign built around prayer, sacrifice, mercy, patient accompaniment and bringing people toward a living relationship with Jesus Christ and His Church. It does not claim that a person can mechanically guarantee another person’s salvation; salvation is God’s gift, freely received.

1. Pray

Intercede daily for one person by name, especially for conversion, healing, reconciliation and final perseverance.

2. Offer

Unite ordinary sacrifices, fasting and suffering to Christ’s Passion for the good of souls.

3. Accompany

Listen without manipulation, witness to Christ, invite to prayer, Scripture, Confession, Mass and parish community.

Words recorded by St. Faustina

“The prayer most pleasing to Me is prayer for the conversion of sinners.” Diary §1397

“My daughter, help Me to save sinners.” Diary §1645

“Know that it is your mission to win souls for Me by prayer and sacrifice.” Diary §1690

A simple 7-day practice

  1. Choose one person and keep the intention confidential.
  2. Offer one decade of the Rosary daily.
  3. Pray the Divine Mercy Chaplet once during the week.
  4. Offer one concrete act of self-denial or service.
  5. Ask the Holy Spirit for wisdom before speaking.
  6. Perform one corporal or spiritual work of mercy.
  7. Entrust the person to Jesus without pressure or anxiety.
