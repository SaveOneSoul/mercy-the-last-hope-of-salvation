Catholic Charismatic Renewal

A dedicated formation page for the history, spirituality, pastoral practice and mission of Catholic Charismatic Renewal, kept distinct from formal Pneumatology and from the prayer-only Baptism in the Holy Spirit page.

Study the RenewalPneumatologyPray for Baptism in the Holy Spirit
Ecclesial principle. Catholic Charismatic Renewal is a current of grace within the Catholic Church. Charisms serve the common good and remain subject to discernment, ecclesial communion, sacramental life and legitimate pastoral authority.

What belongs in Charismatic Renewal

This page gathers the material that previously made the CHARIS page too broad.

History of the Renewal

Duquesne 1967, the growth of Catholic Charismatic Renewal, ICCRS and the establishment of CHARIS.

Baptism in the Holy Spirit

Its place in Catholic renewal as a grace of renewed openness to Christian initiation, not an additional sacrament.

Life in the Spirit Seminars

Kerygma, conversion, healing and forgiveness, Jesus as Lord, the promise of the Spirit, Baptism in the Holy Spirit and continuing growth.

Charisms & discernment

Prophecy, tongues, healing, discernment and other gifts studied through Scripture, Church teaching and responsible pastoral practice.

Prayer groups & communities

Praise and worship, intercession, Scripture, fellowship, accountability, safeguarding and service.

Mission & evangelization

Witness to Jesus, service to the poor, Christian unity, youth ministry and missionary discipleship.

Leadership

Servant leadership, ecclesial obedience, formation, financial transparency, safeguarding and healthy ministry boundaries.
