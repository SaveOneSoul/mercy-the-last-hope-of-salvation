A ten-unit study path

Each lesson includes objectives, original explanatory text, primary-source readings, two knowledge-check questions and a case exercise. Your existing Mariology progress remains available.

  1. Mary in salvation historyRead Marian passages within the story of Christ and Israel.
  2. Theotokos: Mother of GodExplain why the title Mother of God is a statement about Christ.
  3. Perpetual virginityDistinguish Catholic doctrine from claims about human dignity or marriage.
  4. Immaculate ConceptionExplain Mary’s preservation from original sin as a gift of Christ’s grace.
  5. Assumption and Christian hopeConnect Mary’s bodily glorification with Christian resurrection hope.
  6. Mary as New EveUse patristic comparison without treating Mary as an independent saviour.
  7. Mary and the ChurchExplain Mary as a model of faith within the communion of the Church.
  8. Intercession and Christ’s unique mediationDescribe intercession without making Mary a rival to Christ.
  9. Devotion, liturgy and worshipDistinguish veneration of Mary from adoration due to God.
  10. Discernment of private revelationDistinguish public Revelation, devotional accounts and ecclesial discernment.

Current guidance on Marian titles

Read the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith’s Mater Populi fidelis (2025) alongside the Catechism and Lumen Gentium VIII. Devotional popularity alone does not establish a dogma or make every title equally appropriate.
