Psychology: Professional Foundations

A sustained 24-unit study of psychological science, research design, neuroscience, sensation and perception, learning, memory, cognition, motivation, emotion, development, personality, social psychology, psychopathology, assessment, psychotherapy, health psychology, ethics and careful integration with Catholic anthropology.

Professional-style, not a professional qualification. This course is intentionally broad and demanding, but it is non-accredited and does not license anyone to diagnose, counsel, provide psychotherapy or represent themselves as a psychologist.
