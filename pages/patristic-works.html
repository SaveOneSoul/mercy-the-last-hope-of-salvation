Primary Works of the Fathers

Every Father or patristic writer in the Mercy portal now has a primary-source record. Where a verified reusable historical edition is already local, the work opens inside Mercy. Where local vendoring is pending, a public-domain source is linked. Where only modern copyrighted translations are readily available, the limitation is stated instead of copying them.

Source integrity: “Original works” here means the actual writings attributed to the author, not Mercy summaries. English translations are identified by source status. Lost, fragmentary, disputed and pseudonymous materials remain labelled accordingly.
