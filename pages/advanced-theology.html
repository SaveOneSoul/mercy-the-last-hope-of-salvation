Advanced Theology Roadmaps

Fifteen additional 20-credit roadmaps extend the core ten disciplines. These are structured syllabi; individual source-reviewed lesson pages are released only when their primary and Magisterial source sets are ready.

Credibility rule. A roadmap is not counted as completed formation credit until its full lessons, assessments and source sets are published. This page avoids claiming credit for material that is only outlined.
