The agents are AI support tools for adults. They are not licensed counsellors, healthcare providers or emergency services. They can make mistakes. Faith-based reflection is optional, and conversations are not a substitute for professional care or trusted relationships.

When you need a person

Immediate danger or urgent medical need: call local emergency services. In India, call 112. If safe, ask someone trusted nearby to stay with you.

Mental-health support in India: Tele-MANAS · 14416. Outside India, contact your local mental-health or crisis service. This website cannot call for help or monitor your safety.

For ongoing support, contact a qualified local counsellor, psychologist or doctor. Ask about credentials, methods, fees, confidentiality and availability. If you are under 18, reach a trusted adult or suitable youth service; if home is unsafe, seek another safe adult or emergency help.
