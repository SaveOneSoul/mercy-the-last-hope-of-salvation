Saints of the Church

A growing searchable directory with feast days, short original biographies and reliable Catholic reference links.

Link policy updated: saint names now open the stable Vatican News Saint of the Day calendar page for that saint’s feast date, instead of fragile guessed article URLs. A feast-date page may contain more than one saint; select the appropriate saint there. The Roman Martyrology contains thousands of saints and blesseds, so this remains a growing catalogue rather than a claim to reproduce the whole Martyrology.

St. Joseph

Feast: March 19

Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary and foster father of Jesus. The Gospels present him as a just man who obeyed God promptly and protected the Holy Family.

Vatican News: Saints of March 19

St. Peter the Apostle

Feast: June 29

Fisherman called by Christ to lead the Twelve. After his denial he was restored by Jesus and became a principal witness to the Resurrection and shepherd of the Church at Rome.

Vatican News: Saints of June 29

St. Paul the Apostle

Feast: June 29

Former persecutor Saul was converted by the risen Christ and became the great missionary to the Gentiles and author of letters central to the New Testament.

Vatican News: Saints of June 29

St. Mary Magdalene

Feast: July 22

Disciple of Jesus who remained near the Cross and became the first recorded witness to the risen Lord in John’s Gospel.

Early bishop and martyr whose letters strongly witness to unity with the bishop, the Eucharist and fidelity to Christ.
