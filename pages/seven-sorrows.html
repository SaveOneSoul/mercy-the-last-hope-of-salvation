Seven Sorrows of Our Lady

The Servite Rosary of the Seven Sorrows, grounded in the Gospel and prayed with seven septets of Hail Marys.

Servite form: seven groups of seven Hail Marys, each preceded by an Our Father. The Secular Servites describe this structure as seven septets separated by an Our Father.

Opening

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. My God, I am sorry for my sins with all my heart. In choosing to do wrong and failing to do good, I have sinned against You whom I should love above all things. With Your help I intend to repent, avoid sin, and live in Your grace. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

The Seven Sorrows

1. The Prophecy of Simeon

Lk 2:34–35

Mary hears that a sword will pierce her soul.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

2. The Flight into Egypt

Mt 2:13–15

The Holy Family becomes a refugee family to protect the Child Jesus.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

3. The Loss of the Child Jesus in the Temple

Lk 2:41–50

Mary and Joseph search in sorrow for Jesus.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

4. Mary Meets Jesus on the Way to Calvary

cf. Lk 23:27–31

Contemplate the Mother sharing the suffering of her Son.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

5. Mary Stands at the Foot of the Cross

Jn 19:25–30

Mary remains with Jesus through His death.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

6. Mary Receives the Body of Jesus

Jn 19:31–37; cf. Lk 23:50–54

Contemplate the sorrow of the Mother receiving her crucified Son.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

7. Jesus Is Laid in the Tomb

Jn 19:38–42

Mary entrusts her Son to the tomb in faith and hope.

Pray: 1 Our Father + 7 Hail Marys.

In honor of Our Lady’s tears

Pray three Hail Marys, asking for a heart that remembers the Passion of Christ and grows in compassion, repentance and perseverance.

Concluding prayer

O Mother of Sorrows, through the tears you shed and the sufferings you endured in union with your Son, obtain for us the grace to remain faithful to Jesus, to repent sincerely of sin, to comfort those who suffer, and to persevere in hope until death. Amen.
