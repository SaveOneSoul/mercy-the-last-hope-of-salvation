Why I created Mercy

Mercy – The Last Hope of Salvation is a personal Catholic digital mission created to make trustworthy Catholic prayer, formation and devotional resources easier to access. The project brings together Divine Mercy, Sacred Scripture, the Eucharist, Marian devotion, the saints, the Gospel of Life, Catholic Charismatic Renewal and Catholic teaching in one growing platform.

The aim is not to replace the Church, priests, spiritual directors or official Catholic sources, but to help people discover them, pray more deeply and grow in faith.
