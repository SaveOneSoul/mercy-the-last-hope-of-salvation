5 INSTRUCTION • This is a warfare prayer against the spirit of death, untimely and premature death, and witchcraft attacks related to death. • This prayer is to be said for at least 27 days. • You are encouraged to pray with blessed candle daily. After the prayer for each day, don’t put off the candle. • The prayer should be said at night (Midnight is advisable). • Fasting is involved in this prayer. Participants can choose to either fast from 6am – 6pm, 6am – 3pm or 6am – 12pm. If you have health issues, do not fast. • After the prayer, you can do charity to end the prayer. • If you have any question or clarification, click on info@maryourhelp.org to send an email to the admin.

6 SAY ALL THE PRAYERS BELOW EVERY DAY (Don’t skip any day.) (Once you begin to pray, don’t stop until you are done praying. Avoid all kinds of distraction.) Begin with the Precious Blood Chaplet and Litany (Before or during the Chaplet, mention all your personal intentions). (Be audible from this point.) In the name of Jesus Christ and by the authority in His Most Precious Blood, I declare that I shall not die but live to declare the works of God in the land of the living in Jesus name. By the blood of Jesus, I wipe out my names from the register of untimely death, in Jesus name.

Every evil power, oppressing my life through dreams of death, fall down and die, in the name of Jesus Christ. Every evil power of the grave speaking death over my life be silenced by the authority in the Blood of Jesus Christ.

Oh God of mercy deliver my soul from death and keep me alive, satisfy me with long life in the name of Jesus.
