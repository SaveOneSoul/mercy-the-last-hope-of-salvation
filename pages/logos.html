Source-rights and versification gate

The Douay-Rheims 1899 remains the primary Catholic reading text. Hebrew/Aramaic uses the pinned OSHB/WLC production package. Septuagint material uses pinned Swete witnesses under CC BY-SA 4.0 in an isolated ShareAlike partition. Latin uses the pinned public-domain Clementine Vulgate transcription. Logos does not invent OT glosses or Greek/Latin morphology, and it does not silently force source verses into Douay numbering when a validated mapping is absent.
