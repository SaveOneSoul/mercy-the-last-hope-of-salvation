Prayer & Mass Intentions

Send a prayer request to Mercy, ask that a Mass intention be assigned to a verified Catholic priest, or register as a priest willing to receive intentions through the Mercy network.

Important distinction. A prayer request may be shared with multiple Catholic intercessory ministries when you explicitly consent. A Mass intention is different: each requested Mass is tracked as a specific obligation and assigned to a verified priest. Mercy does not collect Mass stipends or offerings in this phase.

Send a prayer intention

Your request goes first to the private Prayer Intentions section in Mercy Admin. If you choose worldwide sharing, your name and intention may also be sent manually to approved Catholic prayer partners.

If you do not select worldwide sharing, the request remains inside Mercy Admin for ministry use. Avoid including unnecessary medical, financial or other highly sensitive personal details about another person without their permission.

Request a Mass intention

Mercy can receive the request and place it in the verified-priest assignment queue. This phase does not collect or transfer Mass offerings.

Canonical safeguard. Mercy treats each requested Mass separately. It does not sell spiritual benefits, and it does not treat a general prayer remembrance as fulfillment of a Mass intention. Any future stipend system must follow canons 945–958 and the 2025 Dicastery for the Clergy decree on Mass intentions.

Priest registration

Catholic priests may register interest in receiving Mass intentions. Registration does not activate the account automatically. Mercy Admin must independently verify the priest through his diocese, religious institute or competent superior before any intention can be assigned.
