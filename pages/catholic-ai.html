Catholic AI — Magisterium Guide

Ask questions about Catholic doctrine, Scripture, the sacraments, prayer, saints, moral teaching, canon law, Divine Mercy and Catholic spiritual life. Answers are requested from Magisterium AI through the private Mercy backend and displayed with the Catholic sources returned by the provider.

Secure architecture: the Magisterium API key belongs only on the Mercy backend. It is never placed in this GitHub Pages site or sent to your browser.
Pastoral and doctrinal boundary. This assistant is a formation aid, not the Magisterium of the Church and not a substitute for a priest, confessor, canon lawyer, physician, counsellor or emergency service. It should distinguish Catholic doctrine and discipline from theological opinion, devotional practice and private revelation. Always follow competent Church authority in individual sacramental or canonical cases.

How the answer is produced

1. Your question

The browser sends the question to the Mercy API. The public page does not contain the Magisterium credential.

2. Catholic AI gateway

The Mercy backend sends the question to Magisterium AI with instructions to remain within Catholic scope and to distinguish doctrine, discipline, opinion, devotion and private revelation.

3. Catholic sources

The page displays the source citations returned by Magisterium so that important claims can be checked against the underlying Catholic documents.
