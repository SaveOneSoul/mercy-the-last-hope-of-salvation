Baptism in the Holy Spirit Prayers

This page is now prayer-only. Theology of the Holy Spirit belongs in Pneumatology; history, formation, charisms and pastoral practice belong in Catholic Charismatic Renewal.

Prayer context. In Catholic Charismatic Renewal, “Baptism in the Holy Spirit” is not an additional sacrament. These prayers ask for renewed surrender to Christ and deeper openness to the grace and action of the Holy Spirit within the sacramental life of the Church.

Prayer for Baptism in the Holy Spirit

Heavenly Father, I praise and thank You for Your love and mercy. In the name of Jesus Christ, Your beloved Son, I surrender myself to You. Lord Jesus, You are my Savior and my Lord. I give You my mind, my heart, my body, my plans, my fears, my gifts and my future. Forgive my sins and renew in me the grace of my Baptism and Confirmation. Father, pour out Your Holy Spirit upon me. Fill me with the love of God. Give me a deeper hunger for Your Word, the Eucharist, prayer, holiness and service. Enkindle in me the gifts You desire for the building up of Your Church. Holy Spirit, lead me into truth, purify my heart, strengthen me against sin, teach me to pray, and make me a faithful witness to Jesus Christ. May everything You give me be used in humility, charity, discernment and obedience to Your will. Mary, Mother of Jesus and Mother of the Church, pray with me for a new Pentecost in my life. Come, Holy Spirit. Renew me and use me for the glory of the Father, through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Come, Holy Spirit

Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and enkindle in them the fire of Your love. Send forth Your Spirit, and they shall be created, and You shall renew the face of the earth. Let us pray: O God, who by the light of the Holy Spirit instructed the hearts of the faithful, grant that by the same Spirit we may be truly wise and ever rejoice in His consolation. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Prayer of Surrender

Jesus, I surrender my life to You. I renounce whatever separates me from You and I choose You again as Lord. Holy Spirit, reveal anything in me that resists the will of God. Give me grace to repent, forgive, trust and obey. Father, make me available for whatever service You desire. Let my prayer, speech, work and relationships bear the fruit of Your Spirit. Amen.

Prayer for the Seven Gifts of the Holy Spirit

O Lord Jesus Christ, who promised to send the Holy Spirit upon Your Apostles and disciples, grant the same Spirit to me. Grant me wisdom to love what is eternal; understanding to receive divine truth; counsel to choose what pleases God; fortitude to carry my cross faithfully; knowledge to see creation in the light of God; piety to delight in His service; and holy fear that keeps me from offending Him. Form in me the mind and heart of a true disciple. Amen.

Prayer After Receiving a Renewed Outpouring

Father, thank You for every grace You have given me. Keep me from seeking experiences for their own sake. Root me in daily prayer, Sacred Scripture, the Eucharist, Reconciliation, charity and communion with the Church. Help me test all things, hold fast to what is good and exercise every gift in love. Holy Spirit, continue Your work in me. Make my life a witness that Jesus Christ is Lord. Amen.

Prayer Before Serving or Praying With Others

Holy Spirit, give me humility, charity and discernment. Keep me from presumption, pressure, manipulation or exaggerated claims. Let me listen well, respect the freedom and dignity of every person, remain within the boundaries of my vocation and ministry, and refer matters requiring pastoral, medical or psychological care to those qualified to help. May Jesus alone be glorified. Amen.
