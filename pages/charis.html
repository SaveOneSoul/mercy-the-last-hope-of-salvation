CHARIS & the Holy Spirit

New Life in the Spirit, Baptism in the Holy Spirit, the Duquesne Weekend, Veni Creator Spiritus and Veni Sancte Spiritus.

CHARIS: the Catholic Charismatic Renewal International Service serves the worldwide Catholic Charismatic Renewal as a current of grace in communion with the Church.

New Life in the Spirit Seminar

CHARIS’s renewed New Life seminar includes nine lessons and testimonies covering God’s love, sin and salvation, healing and forgiveness, faith and conversion, Jesus as Lord, the Holy Spirit, Baptism in the Spirit, and personal/community growth.

Baptism in the Holy Spirit

In Catholic Charismatic spirituality, Baptism in the Holy Spirit is not a new sacrament and does not replace sacramental Baptism or Confirmation. It is a prayerful renewal of baptismal grace and openness to the Holy Spirit, His gifts and mission.

The Duquesne Weekend

From 17–19 February 1967, students at a retreat at The Ark and the Dove near Pittsburgh experienced a powerful outpouring of the Holy Spirit. The event became known as the Duquesne Weekend and is widely remembered as a foundational moment in the modern Catholic Charismatic Renewal.

Veni Creator Spiritus

Latin

Veni, Creator Spiritus, mentes tuorum visita, imple superna gratia, quae tu creasti pectora. Qui diceris Paraclitus, altissimi donum Dei, fons vivus, ignis, caritas, et spiritalis unctio. Tu septiformis munere, digitus paternae dexterae, tu rite promissum Patris, sermone ditans guttura. Accende lumen sensibus, infunde amorem cordibus, infirma nostri corporis virtute firmans perpeti. Hostem repellas longius pacemque dones protinus; ductore sic te praevio vitemus omne noxium. Per te sciamus da Patrem, noscamus atque Filium, teque utriusque Spiritum credamus omni tempore. Deo Patri sit gloria, et Filio, qui a mortuis surrexit, ac Paraclito, in saeculorum saecula. Amen.

English

Come, Creator Spirit; visit the minds of Your faithful. Fill with heavenly grace the hearts that You created. You are called the Advocate, the gift of God Most High, the living fountain, fire, love, and spiritual anointing. You are sevenfold in Your gifts, the finger of the Father’s right hand, the promised One of the Father, enriching our speech with Your word. Kindle light in our senses; pour love into our hearts; strengthen the weakness of our bodies with enduring power. Drive the enemy far away and grant us peace without delay. With You going before us as our guide, may we avoid everything harmful. Through You grant that we may know the Father and also know the Son, and that we may always believe in You, the Spirit of them both. Glory be to God the Father, and to the Son who rose from the dead, and to the Advocate, for ages of ages. Amen.

Veni Sancte Spiritus

Latin

Veni, Sancte Spiritus, et emitte caelitus lucis tuae radium. Veni, pater pauperum, veni, dator munerum, veni, lumen cordium. Consolator optime, dulcis hospes animae, dulce refrigerium. In labore requies, in aestu temperies, in fletu solatium. O lux beatissima, reple cordis intima tuorum fidelium. Sine tuo numine, nihil est in homine, nihil est innoxium. Lava quod est sordidum, riga quod est aridum, sana quod est saucium. Flecte quod est rigidum, fove quod est frigidum, rege quod est devium. Da tuis fidelibus, in te confidentibus, sacrum septenarium. Da virtutis meritum, da salutis exitum, da perenne gaudium. Amen. Alleluia.

English

Come, Holy Spirit, and send from heaven a ray of Your light. Come, Father of the poor; come, giver of gifts; come, light of hearts. Best Consoler, sweet guest of the soul, sweet refreshment. In labor, rest; in heat, relief; in tears, consolation. O most blessed Light, fill the inmost heart of Your faithful. Without Your divine power there is nothing in man, nothing that is innocent. Wash what is unclean; water what is dry; heal what is wounded. Bend what is rigid; warm what is cold; guide what has gone astray. Give to Your faithful, who trust in You, Your sacred sevenfold gift. Give the reward of virtue; give the end of salvation; give everlasting joy. Amen. Alleluia.
