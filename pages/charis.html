Catholic theology

Baptism in the Holy Spirit

In Catholic Charismatic usage, Baptism in the Holy Spirit is not another sacrament. It is a grace of renewal and actualization of what God has already given through Christian initiation, opening the believer more consciously to holiness, mission and charisms.

What it is

A renewed surrender to grace

It commonly includes deeper faith in Jesus as Lord, renewed awareness of the Father’s love, repentance, prayer, love for Scripture and the sacraments, zeal for witness and openness to the Spirit’s gifts.

Scripture: Acts 1:8; Acts 2; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:14–27; 1 Corinthians 12–14.

What it is not

Not an “eighth sacrament”

It neither repeats Baptism nor replaces Confirmation. Sacramental initiation remains foundational. The prayer for an outpouring of the Spirit belongs within the Church’s life of faith, worship and discipleship.

CHARIS doctrinal Q&A

Gifts for service

Charisms, prayer ministry & discernment

The Holy Spirit gives gifts for the common good. Authentic charismatic practice therefore combines expectancy with humility, pastoral accountability and testing.

1. Receive

Ask for the Spirit’s gifts with faith, without treating any charism as a personal possession or sign of superiority.

2. Discern

Test spiritual impressions against Scripture, Catholic faith, the fruits of the Spirit, reason, circumstances and competent pastoral guidance.

3. Serve

Charisms reach maturity when they build up others, strengthen communion, support evangelization and lead to charity.

Prophetic intercession

Prompting is not public revelation

An image, word, burden or impression received in prayer should never be presented as unquestionable divine command. It remains subject to discernment and must respect human freedom.

Laying on of hands

Prayer, not sacramental imitation

CHARIS doctrinal guidance distinguishes ordinary charismatic prayer with laying on of hands from sacramental rites. Permission, sensitivity and pastoral clarity matter.

Stand in the gap

Intercession

Christian intercession participates in Christ’s own prayer for others. The Holy Spirit helps our weakness and teaches the Church to pray for conversion, healing, peace, leaders, the suffering, enemies and the needs of the world.

CHARIS Intercession

CHARIS maintains an international intercession service, while CHARIS India also supports national intercession formation and prayer networks.

CHARIS Intercession Commission

Pastoral boundary

Intercession should never manipulate fear, claim secret authority over another person, promise guaranteed healing or replace sacramental, medical or professional care where these are needed.

Veni Creator Spiritus — English
Come, Creator Spirit; visit the minds of Your faithful. Fill with heavenly grace the hearts that You created. You are called the Advocate, the gift of God Most High, the living fountain, fire, love, and spiritual anointing. You are sevenfold in Your gifts, the finger of the Father’s right hand, the promised One of the Father, enriching our speech with Your word. Kindle light in our senses; pour love into our hearts; strengthen the weakness of our bodies with enduring power. Drive the enemy far away and grant us peace without delay. With You going before us as our guide, may we avoid everything harmful. Through You grant that we may know the Father and also know the Son, and that we may always believe in You, the Spirit of them both. Glory be to God the Father, and to the Son who rose from the dead, and to the Advocate, for ages of ages. Amen.

Prayer for Baptism in the Holy Spirit
God and Father of mercy and love, I thank, I praise and glorify you. You have made everything by the Love in the Holy Spirit. You have fashioned everything both living and non-living thing with Your Holy Spirit. You made the incarnation of Your Son in the womb of the Blessed Virgin Mary through Your Holy Spirit. You consecrated the Bread and wine and transformed them into Your Son's Body and Blood through Your Holy Spirit,You raised Him from the dead through Your Holy Spirit. Therefore, oh Holy Father, I implore you through the Holy and Mighty Name of Jesus Your Son that You cloth me, those on whose behalf I pray, my family members and the whole Church at large with Power from Above and grant us (me) the same anointing and baptism of the Holy Spirit as You have granted to the Blessed Virgin and Your holy apostles in the upper room in Jerusalem. Again, oh Father, fulfill, now, in our days, What You have accomplished when the Gospel was first preached on earth. Break our hearts of stone and write Your Holy Word into our hearts as You Holy Father have written in the hearts of Your holy apostles on the day of Pentecost. May we testify Your love and mercy with deeds and power and most of all with my own life. Whenever I proclaim Your Word, Your Mercy and Call on The Mighty and Matchless Name of Jesus Your beloved Son, may You stretch out Your mighty and powerful hands to pour out Your Spirit, to save, to Renew, to refine, to forgive, to heal to touch the hearts of sinners, to cast out demons and darkness and to restore peace and orderliness in the world. Anoint my words oh Lord, my preaching,my prayers and intercessions and grant me that wondrous power that I may be able to do wonders and save many souls and whenever I call on Your Name on behalf of some Sinners through the merits of the Sorrowful Passion of Your Beloved Son, may You hear from heaven to save those on whose behalf I intercede. Grant me the power and authority over satan and demons, Sicknesses, natural calamities, by sending down Your Holy Spirit upon me like the dew fall. Grant Oh Lord to Your Servant to preach Your words with all boldness while You stretch out Your Hands to heal and signs and wonders are performed through the Name of Your Holy Servant, Jesus (Acts). And May all Your people come to know Your Son in the Eucharist, and love Him as their own lives.

Structured formation

CHARIS & Life in the Holy Spirit — 20 credits

This course organizes the page into ten 2-credit units for deeper formation and guided study.

CHARIS & Life in the Holy Spirit10 units · 2 credits each
Credit note. These are internal Save One Soul formation credits. They are not university, seminary, ECTS or government-accredited credits.
12 credits

The kerygma and personal encounter with Christ

Core reading: Mark 1:14–15; John 3:16; Acts 2:22–41

Study proclamation of God's love, sin, salvation in Christ, conversion, faith and discipleship as the foundation of renewal.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

22 credits

Christian initiation and Baptism in the Holy Spirit

Core reading: Acts 1–2; Romans 6; CCC 1210–1321; CHARIS doctrinal guidance

Distinguish the sacraments of initiation from the charismatic prayer for renewed openness to the grace already received.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

32 credits

The Holy Spirit in Scripture and the Church

Core reading: John 14–16; Romans 8; Dominum et Vivificantem

Trace the Spirit's mission in Christ, Pentecost, sanctification, prayer, mission and ecclesial life.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

42 credits

Charisms for the common good

Core reading: 1 Corinthians 12–14; Romans 12; Lumen Gentium 12

Study charisms as gifts for service, not status, and distinguish them from holiness or sacramental office.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

52 credits

Discernment of spirits

Core reading: 1 Thessalonians 5:19–22; 1 John 4:1; Ignatian tradition

Learn doctrinal, moral, pastoral and prudential criteria for testing impressions, prophecy and spiritual experiences.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

62 credits

Praise, worship and prayer ministry

Core reading: Psalms; Ephesians 5:18–20; 1 Corinthians 14

Study praise, spontaneous prayer, laying on of hands, prayer teams, pastoral boundaries and ecclesial accountability.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

72 credits

Healing, deliverance and pastoral safeguards

Core reading: James 5:13–16; Gospel healing narratives; Catechism on sacramentals

Study prayer for healing without promises, manipulation or replacement of medical, psychological or sacramental care.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

82 credits

Intercession and spiritual mission

Core reading: Romans 8:26–27; 1 Timothy 2:1–6; Ephesians 6:10–18

Develop disciplined intercession for Church, world, conversion, leaders, suffering people and evangelization.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

92 credits

Communion, ecumenism and service to the poor

Core reading: John 17; Acts 2:42–47; Unitatis Redintegratio; Matthew 25

Study CHARIS priorities of Christian unity, communion and service to people in need.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

102 credits

Leadership, mission and formation of communities

Core reading: Matthew 28:18–20; Acts 13; Evangelii Nuntiandi; CHARIS mission

Integrate prayer groups, youth ministry, catechesis, evangelization, safeguarding, accountability and missionary discipleship.

Study task: Read the primary sources, write structured notes, answer one doctrinal question, and record pastoral applications and cautions.

Completion pattern

10 units × 2 credits = 20 credits. Suggested evidence: ten study notes, one Scripture study, one source-analysis paper, one discernment case study, and one ministry/mission project.
