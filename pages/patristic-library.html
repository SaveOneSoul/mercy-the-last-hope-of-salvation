Patristic Primary Text Library

Use the Fathers study notes together with the primary texts. This library points to complete public-domain translations where available, surviving fragments where a work is lost, and older scholarly editions for textual research.

Local-library policy: public-domain patristic editions are vendored into this repository so the normal reading experience stays inside Mercy. Modern copyrighted translations are not copied without permission. Lost works remain explicitly fragmentary.

Complete public-domain edition

The Didache

The complete public-domain Didache edition is included in the local import plan and is presented as an anonymous apostolic-era Church manual. Its chapters cover the Two Ways, Baptism, fasting, prayer, Eucharistic thanksgiving, itinerant ministry, local bishops and deacons, and eschatological vigilance.

Local Origen corpus

Origen: De Principiis, Contra Celsum, letters and textual study

Mercy now has a vendoring pipeline for the 1869 and 1872 public-domain Origen volumes. These include De Principiis, letters, and Contra Celsum Books I–VIII. The reader keeps Origen’s speculative passages distinct from later Catholic doctrinal reception.

Origen Volume IOrigen Volume II

The Hexapla

The original six-column Hexapla is lost. Therefore Mercy will never label a modern reconstruction as the complete Hexapla. The corpus manifest reserves a separate fragmentary-text collection for the surviving readings and the public-domain Field reconstruction.

First local import tranche

Apostolic Fathers collection

The first source-locked volume contains 1 Clement, 2 Clement, Polycarp, the Martyrdom of Polycarp, Barnabas, the Ignatian letter collection in the historical edition, the Epistle to Diognetus, the Shepherd of Hermas and fragments of Papias. These are imported into local structured sections for the reader.
