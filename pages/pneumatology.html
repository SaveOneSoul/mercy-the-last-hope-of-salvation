Core doctrine

The Holy Spirit in Catholic theology

The Holy Spirit is God

Study the Spirit as the third Person of the Most Holy Trinity, consubstantial with the Father and the Son.

Scripture: Matthew 28:19; Acts 5:3–4; 2 Corinthians 13:13.

Procession and Trinitarian life

Study the eternal origin of the Holy Spirit and the Western and Eastern theological language surrounding procession.

The Spirit in salvation history

Creation, prophecy, the Incarnation, Christ's ministry, Paschal Mystery and Pentecost.

Scripture: Genesis 1:2; Luke 1:35; Luke 4:18; John 14–16; Acts 2.

Sanctification

The Spirit indwells the baptized, forms Christ in the faithful, gives grace, produces His fruits and leads the Church into holiness.

Scripture: Romans 8; Galatians 5:16–25.

Charisms

Charisms are gifts given for the common good. Their exercise belongs within charity, discernment, order and ecclesial communion.

Scripture: Romans 12; 1 Corinthians 12–14.

The Spirit and the Church

The Spirit vivifies the Church, sustains her mission, gives unity in diversity, assists her worship and empowers witness.
